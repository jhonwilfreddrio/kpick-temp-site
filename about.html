About K-Pick Trading Corp

Korean medical device distributor — Manila, Philippines — founded 2020

K-Pick Trading Corp is a Manila-based B2B medical supplies distributor founded in 2020. We import and distribute Korean medical devices for hospitals, clinics, pharmacies, resellers and healthcare procurement officers across the Philippines, and we are the exclusive Philippine distributor for three Korean manufacturers: Sungshim (syringes, needles and infusion sets), EROP (laparoscopic and surgical devices) and INSUFiNE (insulin pen needles by Tae-Chang Industrial).

Who is accountable

K-Pick Trading Corp holds a Philippine FDA License to Operate as a medical device distributor. Regulatory questions, certificate verification and Certificate of Product Registration (CPR) status per SKU are handled by our compliance desk at compliance@kpicktradingcorp.com. Manufacturer certifications (ISO 13485, CE, Korean MFDS/KGMP) are published on each brand page: Sungshim, EROP, INSUFiNE.

Milestones

  • 2020 — K-Pick Trading Corp founded in Manila; began distributing Korean insulin pen needles.
  • 2023 — Appointed exclusive Philippine distributor for INSUFiNE.
  • 2024 — Appointed exclusive Philippine distributor for Sungshim.
  • 2025 — Appointed exclusive Philippine distributor for EROP.

What we do

  • Medical device distribution and importation from Korea
  • Wholesale and dealer supply for hospitals, clinics, pharmacies and resellers
  • Logistics and fulfilment from Manila to Luzon, Visayas and Mindanao
  • Quotation, purchase-order and after-sales coordination for procurement teams

Where we deliver

Orders are coordinated from our Malate, Manila office and shipped nationwide: Metro Manila, Luzon, Visayas and Mindanao. Delivery lead time depends on stock availability and destination; procurement teams can contact us for a region-specific quote.

Mission, vision, approach

Mission — reliable Korean healthcare products for the Philippine market. Vision — a premier medical supplies distributor built on trust and growth. Approach — long-term partnerships with professional operational support. Building Partnerships. Delivering Quality. Improving Lives.
