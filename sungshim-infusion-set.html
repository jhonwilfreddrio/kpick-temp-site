Sungshim IV Infusion Sets (Standard, Y-Type, Air-Vented)

The Infusion Set (I.V. Set) is a sterile disposable medical device designed for the controlled administration of intravenous fluids, medications, and blood products to patients in hospital and clinic settings. It supports safe, contamination-reduced fluid delivery for use by trained healthcare professionals.

Sungshim's infusion set range includes standard sets, air-vent cap sets, Y-site sets, flow regulator sets, extension tubes, and specialty filter and regulator sets, giving healthcare facilities configuration options suited to different fluid administration needs.

Instructions for Use

  1. Preparation
    • Inspect the packaging for any damage, missing parts, or expired products before use.
    • Wash hands thoroughly before handling the infusion set.
    • Hang the IV bag or bottle securely and spike it using the provided connector.
  2. Usage Procedure
    • Prime the drip chamber and tubing to remove all air bubbles before connecting to the patient.
    • Adjust the roller clamp or flow regulator to achieve the prescribed drip rate.
    • Connect the infusion set to the patient's vascular access device (IV cannula or port).
    • Monitor the infusion site for signs of infiltration or phlebitis throughout administration.
    • Observe the IV bag level and flow rate continuously to ensure proper fluid delivery.
    • After infusion is complete, safely disconnect the set from the patient following facility protocol.

Disposal and Storage After Use

  • This product is intended for single use only, do not reuse.
  • Dispose of used infusion sets in a sealed, puncture-resistant sharps container and follow proper disposal regulations.

Safety Information

Warnings

  • Do not use if the packaging is damaged, opened, or contaminated.
  • Do not use if any component is cracked, leaking, or otherwise defective.

Precautions

  • Use only for its intended purpose.
  • Always prime the tubing to remove all air bubbles before connecting to the patient; residual air poses a risk of air embolism.
  • Do not exceed the prescribed flow rate.
  • Monitor the infusion site regularly for signs of infiltration or phlebitis.
  • This is a single-use sterile product; reuse is strictly prohibited.
  • Users must be trained in proper use and handling before operating this device.

Intended Use

This product is intended for the controlled delivery of intravenous fluids, medications, or blood products from a fluid container to a patient via a vascular access device, for use by trained healthcare professionals in hospital and clinic settings.

Product Specifications

  • Shelf Life: 3 years from the date of manufacture
  • Packaging: Individual sterile packaging by listed model and configuration variant
  • Storage Conditions: Store at room temperature; avoid exposure to direct sunlight, heat, and humidity
